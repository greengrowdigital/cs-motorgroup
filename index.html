Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an appointment for a detail?
An appointment guarantees your slot, but walk-ins are welcome and we can usually take a Gold package the same day. Platinum packages and ceramic coatings need to be scheduled because the car stays with us longer.
How long does a ceramic coating take?
Plan on the car staying with us for one to two days. The paint has to be washed, decontaminated, corrected and wiped down before the coating goes on, and then it needs to cure before the car goes back out in the weather.
What do I need to rent a car?
You must be 25 or older with a valid driver's license, proof of insurance and a credit card in your own name. Exotic tier rentals carry a higher security deposit, which we release when the vehicle comes back.
Are the used cars inspected?
Yes. Every vehicle we list goes through our own service bay first, including a mechanical inspection, a vehicle history check and a road test. It is also fully detailed before delivery.
Do you offer financing or take trade-ins?
Both. We work with several lenders across the credit spectrum and we will appraise your current vehicle on the spot. Bring the car and the payoff information and we can structure the deal in one visit.
Is pickup and drop-off really free?
Within 5 miles of the shop, yes, on any service booking. Beyond that we charge a flat mileage fee that we quote before you confirm the appointment.
